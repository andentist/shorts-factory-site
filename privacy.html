Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Shorts Factory ("the tool") is a personal tool run by its owner on their own computer. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Data we access

  • Basic channel information of the signed-in account (channel ID and channel name), to confirm uploads go to the correct channel.
  • The owner's own videos and their statistics (views, likes, comments, average view duration, average view percentage, subscribers gained), to evaluate which topics perform well.
  • Public search results and public statistics of popular Shorts on YouTube, to identify trends.
  • Permission to upload videos to the signed-in channel.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to produce and publish the owner's own videos and to improve their future videos.
  • OAuth tokens and analysis results are stored only on the owner's own computer, in local files. Nothing is stored on any server operated by the tool.
  • Video titles and statistics may be sent to an AI model provider (Anthropic) solely to generate scripts and analysis for the owner.
  • No data is sold, shared for advertising, or disclosed to any other third party.
  • Stored statistics are refreshed or deleted within 30 days, and the owner can delete all stored data at any time by deleting the local data folder.

Cookies

The tool is a command-line program and does not use cookies or place any information on users' devices or browsers.

Revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google security settings page).
