Shorts Factory

Shorts Factory is a personal, self-hosted command-line tool that the channel owner runs on their own computer to publish short educational videos to their own YouTube channels.

What it does

  • Writes a short script with an AI model, creates narration and subtitles, and combines them with royalty-free stock footage into a vertical video.
  • Uploads the finished video to the owner's own channel with the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), after the owner signs in with Google OAuth.
  • Reads the owner's own video statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API) and public search results for popular Shorts, to decide which topics to make next.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool is not offered to other users, has no sign-up, and does not access any channel other than the ones the owner signs in with.
